Current Trends

Three door styles defining the modern kitchen.

Style 01

Micro Shaker

The Micro Shaker is a modern cabinet door style featuring shaker detailing only along the outer ¼" of the door edge. This design highlights the beautiful wood grain on the center panel while providing a durable edge for lasting quality.

Style 02

Double Shaker

The Double Shaker takes us back to when quality was a focus in life. The art deco style of the stepped shaker look has come back to give a luxury look to any space.

Style 03

Fluted Panel

Fluted panels have become the epitome of luxury and modern design. Whether they are used throughout a space or as a thoughtful accent, they bring an elevated sense of sophistication and style to any setting.
